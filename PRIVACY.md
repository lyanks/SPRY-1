# Spry - Privacy Policy

Spry is a student project (a meeting-analytics demo built for a university course).

**What we collect.** When you sign in, we receive your email address (and, if you use
Google, your name and profile name) from Amazon Cognito and Google. If you sign in with
a password, we receive the email and password you type; the password is handled and
stored (hashed) by Amazon Cognito, not by Spry.

**What we use it for.** Only to let you sign in and to show your email in the page header.
We do not sell, share or use it for advertising or analytics.

**Where it is stored.** In an Amazon Cognito user pool in Amazon Web Services (AWS).
Meeting data you enter in Spry is stored in a PostgreSQL database on AWS.

**How long.** Until the demo is taken down, or until you ask us to delete your account.

**Your choices.** You can ask for your account and data to be deleted at any time by
contacting the project owner at: <YOUR EMAIL HERE>.

Last updated: October 2026.
